## 1. Overall Summary
Sprint 2 delivers a major expansion in operational master data management, implementing fully interactive and workable screens for **Customer Management**, **Product Catalog Management**, **Effective-Dated Price History**, and **Defensive Deletion & Integrity Guards**. 

The Customer Management interface provides a high-density master data grid with real-time compound status tabs (`Active`, `Inactive`, `All`), dynamic debounce search, live record counts, and granular audit provenance stamps (`CREATED`, `EDITED`, `DELETED`, `RESTORED`). The Add and Edit Customer dialogs feature full vertical form stacks with auto-generated customer identifiers (`C-006` / `C0005`), structured street/city address inputs, and an interactive Payment Terms dropdown supporting standard commercial terms (`Net 15`, `Net 30`, `Net 45`, `Net 60`, `COD`, `Prepaid`) alongside custom term entry.

The Product Management module implements a decoupled commercial pricing architecture where product identity (code, description, unit of measure) is strictly isolated from temporal financial rates. The master catalog table displays derived current unit prices calculated dynamically from price history schedules. Clicking "Prices" opens an effective-dated Price History sub-ledger modal with independent status filtering (`Active`, `Inactive`, `All`), inline price auditing, and an integrated "Add Price" nested dialog with datepicker and monetary formatting. Price additions, edits, soft-deletions, and restorations immediately recalculate parent catalog prices in real time.

Defensive UX principles are enforced throughout the system:
1. **Reversible Soft Deletion (`ConfirmDeleteModal`):** Transparently warns operators with an amber alert callout that the entity will be flagged as `Inactive` and hidden from operational team members while remaining restorable by administrators.
2. **Referential Integrity Guard (`BlockedDeleteModal`):** Automatically detects active child dependencies (e.g., active sales orders linked to Acme Corporation `C-001`, active price tiers or sales lines referencing `P-001`) and halts deletion with detailed diagnostic copy, preserving audit integrity.
3. **Comprehensive Empty & Error States (`EmptyState`):** Preserves page context, navigation, and toolbar controls during zero-match search queries, empty filter segments, or empty initial tables, offering contextual recovery actions (`Clear search filter`, `Show all records`, `Add Customer` / `Add Product`).
4. **Dark Mode Readability:** Fully remediated high-contrast color mappings and resolved the `--white` variable inheritance issue across all landing headers, table rows, badges, inputs, and modal dialogs.
