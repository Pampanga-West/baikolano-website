# Hope, Inc. ERP 

Hope, Inc. ERP is a small business system that keeps customers, products, employees and sales in one place. “ERP” stands for Enterprise Resource Planning: instead of separate spreadsheets for each department, every module reads and writes the same database. In practice, it acts as the central digital hub for a company's daily operations. It allows a business team to track buyer details, manage product catalogs and changing prices, record employee job histories, and process sales transactions all within a single workspace. Because all this information is connected, the system automatically calculates totals and generates printable business reports, such as identifying top customers or tracking total revenue, giving managers a clear, real-time picture of how the company is performing.
